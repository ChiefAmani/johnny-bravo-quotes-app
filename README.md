# Johnny Bravo Quotes App

## Whoa, Mama! Get Your Daily Dose of Johnny Bravo!

Tired of searching the internet for that perfect Johnny Bravo line? Wanna relive the glory days of 90s cartoons with a quick hit of the King of Cool? This app's got your back, baby! We've packed over 50 of Johnny's most iconic, hilarious, and sometimes questionable quotes into one slick, retro-styled web app.

It's pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript – no fuss, no muss, just pure Bravo power. Perfect for a quick laugh, a nostalgic trip, or showing off your impeccable taste in cartoons.

## Why You Need This, Baby! (Features)

*   **Instant Johnny Fix:** Hit the "Random Quote" button and get a fresh dose of Bravo wisdom. Never be without a classic line again!
*   **Find Your Faves:** Got a specific quote in mind? Our search bar lets you filter through all the quotes faster than Johnny can flex.
*   **Copy & Paste Like a Pro:** See a quote you love? Just click it to copy it straight to your clipboard. Share the Bravo love with ease!
*   **Know Your Numbers:** A handy counter keeps track of how many awesome quotes are at your fingertips.
*   **Retro Vibes, Modern Moves:** We're talkin' classic yellow and black, bold fonts, and animations that scream 90s cartoon. Plus, it looks great on your phone, tablet, or desktop – Johnny's always ready for his close-up!
*   **Zero Drama Deployment:** This is a single `index.html` file. Drop it on GitHub Pages, and you're live. Easy peasy, lemon squeezy.

## How to Get Your Bravo On (Usage)

1.  **See a Quote:** The main display shows a random quote when you load the page.
2.  **New Quote, Who Dis?:** Click "Random Quote" for another gem.
3.  **Search & Conquer:** Type into the search bar to filter the list and find specific quotes.
4.  **Copy That!:** Click on any quote (in the main display or the list) to copy it to your clipboard. A little "Copied!" message will pop up to confirm.
5.  **Browse the List:** Scroll through the full list of quotes below the controls.

## Deploy It Yourself, Pretty Mama! (Deployment to GitHub Pages)

Want to host your own Johnny Bravo shrine? It's simpler than trying to get a date with Johnny. This app is built for static site deployment, perfect for GitHub Pages.

1.  **Fork This Repo:** Head over to `ChiefAmani/johnny-bravo-quotes-app` and fork it to your own GitHub account.
2.  **Enable GitHub Pages:**
    *   Go to your forked repository on GitHub.
    *   Click the **"Settings"** tab.
    *   In the left sidebar, find **"Pages"** under "Code and automation."
    *   Under "Build and deployment," set "Source" to **"Deploy from a branch."**
    *   For "Branch," select `main` (or your primary branch) and choose the `/(root)` folder.
    *   Click **"Save."**
3.  **Flex Your New Site:** Give GitHub a few minutes to work its magic. Your app will then be live at `https://YOUR_USERNAME.github.io/johnny-bravo-quotes-app/`.
    *(Remember to replace `YOUR_USERNAME` with your actual GitHub username!)*
